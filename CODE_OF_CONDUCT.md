## Code of Conduct  
  
QuizMaster is a learning project for students and first-time contributors.  
We want everyone to feel welcome and safe here.  
  
## Our promise  
  
Everyone who takes part in this project — in issues, pull requests, and  
discussions — should be treated with respect, no matter their experience  
level, background, or identity.  
  
## Expected behavior  
  
- Be kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to beginners.  
- Give helpful and constructive feedback.  
- Accept feedback on your own work gracefully.  
- Remember that many contributors are students learning for the first time.  
  
## Unacceptable behavior  
  
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.  
- Racist, sexist, or otherwise discriminatory language.  
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.  
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.  
  
## Reporting a problem  
  
If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the  
maintainers at: psm@nxtwave.in 
  
All reports will be reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may warn  
the person, remove comments, or block them from the project if needed.  
  
## Scope  
  
This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests,  
discussions, and any other place where people represent this project.
